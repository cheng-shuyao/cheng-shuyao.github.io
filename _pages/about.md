Cheng, Shuyao is an Associate Professor at the [Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.ict.ac.cn/) (ICT-CAS). He received his B.S. degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2019 and his Ph.D. from ICT-CAS in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Yunji Chen. His research focuses on processor design automation. His representative work, the QiMeng-CPU series, was highlighted by [Nature News](https://www.nature.com/) as “the first chip to be designed by AI” and “good news for Chinese chip development.” He has received several awards, including the World Internet Conference Leading Science and Technology Award, the Huang Kun–Cambricon Elite Award, the CCF Doctoral Dissertation Incentive Program (Technical Committee on Computer Architecture), and the CCF Distinguished Paper Award. He is also a silver medalist at the National Intellectual Games of China.
